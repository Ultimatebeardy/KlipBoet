# KlipBoet: stream clips to shorts, automatically

KlipBoet turns your OBS replay clips into ready-to-post 9:16 shorts for
YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels, automatically.

Press your clip key on stream and KlipBoet builds the short for you: facecam and
gameplay layout, hook text, your branding, intro and end cards. Review it, tweak it,
post it.

## Download
Grab the latest installer from **Releases** (on the right).
Windows 10/11, 64-bit. Works with OBS Studio (replay buffer) or a replay folder.
